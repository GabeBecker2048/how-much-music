All counts are exact (arbitrary-precision integers); scientific values are rounded to four significant digits. One note at a time, with no ties. Triplet groups, when enabled, are counted as whole groups. See the paper and addendum for the method.

The solution is Gabriel Becker's, worked out by hand as a math proof and a CS proof with pseudocode. Claude Opus 5.5 translated it into the formal paper and built the calculator.
